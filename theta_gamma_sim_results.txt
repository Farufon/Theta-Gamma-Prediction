THE SIGNATURE, in one sentence
============================================================================
Standard QM: after proper-time compensation the CHSH value returns
to 2*sqrt(2) regardless of H and t.
Theta-Gamma: a residual deficit remains, linear in g*H/c^2,
saturating in t with timescale tau_sat, and NO compensation
protocol removes it, because stabilization was never unitary.

That saturation knee is the fingerprint. Ordinary decoherence
grows without bound; this plateaus. An experimentalist can tell
them apart.
